HealthSecure Insurance - Claims Guide

1. How Claims are Submitted
In-network providers submit claims automatically. For out-of-network care, members submit a CMS-1500 or standard claim form with itemized receipts.

2. Claim Lifecycle & Statuses
Lifecycle steps: Received -> Pending Review -> Approved/Denied -> Processed.
Check status online or call support listed in 01_Member_Handbook.pdf.

3. Common Denial Reasons & Processing
Denials occur due to missing authorization (04_Prior_Authorization_Guide.pdf), non-covered services (03_Coverage_Policies.pdf), or filing after 90 days. Processing takes 14–30 calendar days.
